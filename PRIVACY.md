# Privacy Policy – EH Link Corrector

EH Link Corrector does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.
It makes no network requests of its own.

The extension stores your eHub tenant name(s) and an on/off setting in
`chrome.storage.sync` (synced by your browser account, if sync is enabled).
It reads the address of pages you navigate to only to recognise
ServiceTitan GO links and eHub sites; this information never leaves your browser.

This is an unofficial tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by ServiceTitan.
